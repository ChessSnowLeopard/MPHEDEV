# 打包全过程

- $X_m[j]$:第m个样本的第j个特征

- 假设有t维特征，n张图像（即一个样本是一个$t\times 1$的向量）

- $W_{t'\times t}$：权重矩阵，t‘表示本层神经元数量，t表示上层神经元（特征数）

- 密文slot：s

- 每张图像取k个特征与其余图像打包，则一个密文中包含s/k个样本（我们可以设batchSize为s/k）

  ## 前向传播

  每张图像取k个特征，每个密文有s/k个特征，则n张图像我们共需要nk/s个密文

  对于一个batch，取以下为一组，一个batch共有t/k个密文
  $$
  X_0[0],X_1[0]...X_{s/k-1}[0]|X_0[1],X_1[1]...X_{s/k-1}[1]|......|X_{0}[k-1],X_1[k-1]...X_{s/k-1}[k-1]
  \\
  \odot
  \\
  W_{0,0},W_{0,0}...W_{0,0}|W_{0,1},W_{0,1}...W_{0,1}|......|W_{0,k-1},W_{0,k-1}...W_{0,k-1}
  \\
  =Z_{0,0:k-1}
  \\
  表示一个bacth的前k-1个特征与权重矩阵的第0行前k列相乘
  $$

  $$
  X_0[k],X_1[k]...X_{s/k-1}[k]|X_0[k+1],X_1[k+1]...X_{s/k-1}[k+1]|......|X_{0}[2k-1],X_1[2k-1]...X_{s/k-1}[2k-1]
  \\
  \odot
  \\
  W_{0,k},W_{0,k}...W_{0,k}|W_{0,k+1},W_{0,k+1}...W_{0,k+1}|......|W_{0,2k-1},W_{0,2k-1}...W_{0,2k-1}
  \\
  =Z_{0,k:2k-1}
  \\
  表示一个bacth的k:2k-1个特征与权重矩阵的第0行k:2k-1列相乘
  $$

  $\vdots$
  $$
  X_0[t-k-1]...X_{s/k-1}[t-k-1]|X_0[t-k],X_1[t-k]...X_{s/k-1}[t-k]|......|X_{0}[t-1],X_1[t-1]...X_{s/k-1}[t-1]
  \\
  \odot
  \\
  W_{0,t-k-1},W_{0,t-k-1}...W_{0,t-k-1}|W_{0,t-k},W_{0,t-k}...W_{0,t-k}|......|W_{0,t-1},W_{0,t-1}...W_{0,t-1}
  \\
  =Z_{0,t-k-1:t-1}
  \\
  表示一个bacth的最后k个特征与权重矩阵的第0行t-k-1:t-1列相乘
  $$
  以上为一个batch的图像与权重矩阵的第一行相乘的结果
  
  上述密文进行$\oplus$操作后进行logk次旋转，得到以下结果即第0个隐藏层神经元的输出，$Z_m^j$表示第m张图像在第j个神经元的输出
  $$
  Z_0^0,Z_1^0,Z_2^0...Z_{s/k-1}^0|Z_0^0,Z_1^0,Z_2^0...Z_{s/k-1}^0|...|Z_0^0,Z_1^0,Z_2^0...Z_{s/k-1}^0
  $$
  同理得其余t‘-1个神经元的输出如下
  $$
  Z_0^1,Z_1^1,Z_2^1...Z_{s/k-1}^1|Z_0^1,Z_1^1,Z_2^1...Z_{s/k-1}^1|...|Z_0^1,Z_1^1,Z_2^1...Z_{s/k-1}^1
  $$
  
  $$
  Z_0^2,Z_1^2,Z_2^2...Z_{s/k-1}^2|Z_0^2,Z_1^2,Z_2^2...Z_{s/k-1}^2|...|Z_0^2,Z_1^2,Z_2^2...Z_{s/k-1}^2
  $$
  
  $\vdots$
  $$
  Z_0^{t'-1},Z_1^{t'-1},Z_2^{t'-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}|Z_0^{t'-1},Z_1^{t'-1},Z_2^{t'-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}|...|Z_0^{t'-1},Z_1^{t'-1},Z_2^{t'-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}
  $$
  生成掩码将上述密文进行重组
  $$
  m_0=[111...111|000...000|000...000|......|000...000]
  \\
  m_1=[000...000|111...111|000...000｜......|000...000]
  \\
  \vdots
  \\
  m_k=[000...000|000...000|000...000|......|111...111]
  $$
  每个密文乘对应的掩码得以下结果
  $$
  Z_0^0,Z_1^0,Z_2^0...Z_{s/k-1}^0|Z_0^1,Z_1^1,Z_2^1...Z_{s/k-1}^1|...|Z_0^{k-1},Z_1^{k-1},Z_2^{k-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{k-1}
  $$
  
  $$
  Z_0^k,Z_1^k,Z_2^k...Z_{s/k-1}^k|Z_0^{k+1},Z_1^{k+1},Z_2^{k+1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{k+1}|...|Z_0^{2k-1},Z_1^{2k-1},Z_2^{2k-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{2k-1}
  $$
  
  $\vdots$
  $$
  Z_0^{t-k-1},Z_1^{t-k-1},Z_2^{t-k-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{t-k-1}|Z_0^{t-k},Z_1^{t-k},Z_2^{t-k}...Z_{s/k-1}^{t-k}|...|Z_0^{t-1},Z_1^{t-1},Z_2^{t-1}...Z_{s/k-1}^{t-1}
  $$
  过激活函数之后：
  $$
  A_0^0,A_1^0,A_2^0...A_{s/k-1}^0|A_0^1,A_1^1,A_2^1...A_{s/k-1}^1|...|A_0^{k-1},A_1^{k-1},A_2^{k-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{k-1}
  $$
  
  $$
  A_0^k,Z_1^k,A_2^k...A_{s/k-1}^k|A_0^{k+1},A_1^{k+1},A_2^{k+1}...A_{s/k-1}^{k+1}|...|A_0^{2k-1},A_1^{2k-1},A_2^{2k-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{2k-1}
  $$
  
  $\vdots$
  $$
  A_0^{t'-1},A_1^{t'-1},A_2^{t'-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}|A_0^{t'-1},A_1^{t'-1},A_2^{t'-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}|...|A_0^{t'-1},A_1^{t'-1},A_2^{t'-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}
  $$
  
  
  ==为简便权重矩阵的表示与上一层相同==
  $$
  A_0^0,A_1^0,A_2^0...A_{s/k-1}^0|A_0^1,A_1^1,A_2^1...A_{s/k-1}^1|...|A_0^{k-1},A_1^{k-1},A_2^{k-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{k-1}
  \\
  \odot
  \\
  W_{0,0},W_{0,0}...W_{0,0}|W_{0,1},W_{0,1}...W_{0,1}|......|W_{0,k-1},W_{0,k-1}...W_{0,k-1}
  \\
  =
  O_{0,0:k-1}
  \\
  表示隐藏层->输出层的权重矩阵第0行前k列与隐藏层前k个神经元的输出计算的结果
  $$
  
  $$
  A_0^k,A_1^k,A_2^k...A_{s/k-1}^k|A_0^{k+1},A_1^{k+1},A_2^{k+1}...A_{s/k-1}^{k+1}|...|A_0^{2k-1},A_1^{2k-1},A_2^{k-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{2k-1}
  \\
  \odot
  \\
  W_{0,k},W_{0,k}...W_{0,k}|W_{0,k+1},W_{0,k+1}...W_{0,k+1}|......|W_{0,k+1},W_{0,k+1}...W_{0,k+1}
  \\
  =
  O_{0,k:2k-1}
  \\
  $$
  
  $$
  \vdots
  $$
  
  
  $$
  A_0^{t'-k-1},A_1^{t'-k-1},A_2^{t'-k-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{t'-k-1}|A_0^{t'-k},A_1^{t'-k},A_2^{t'-k}...A_{s/k-1}^{t'-k}|...|A_0^{t'-1},A_1^{t'-1},A_2^{t'-1}...A_{s/k-1}^{t'-1}
  \\
  \odot
  \\
  W_{0,t'-k-1},W_{0,t'-k-1}...W_{0,t'-k-1}|W_{0,t'-k},W_{0,t'-k}...W_{0,t'-k}|......|W_{0,t'-1},W_{0,t'-1}...W_{0,t'-1}
  \\
  =
  O_{0,t'-k-1:t'-1}
  \\
  $$
  
  
  

以上共有t'/k个，同上一层，$\oplus$后旋转，得到输出层第0个神经元的output，$O_m^j$表示第m个样本在第j个神经元的输出
$$
O_0^0,O_1^0,O_2^0...O_{s/k-1}^0|O_0^0,O_1^0,O_2^0...O_{s/k-1}^0|......|O_0^0,O_1^0,O_2^0...O_{s/k-1}^0
$$
这样的共有t''个

输出层我们使用softmax函数计算，$p_i=\frac{e^{O^i}}{\sum e^{O^i}}$

对输出层的t‘’个密文进行指数计算
$$
e^{O_0^0},e^{O_1^0}...e^{O_{s/k-1}^0}|...|e^{O_0^0},e^{O_1^0}...e^{O_{s/k-1}^0}
$$

$$
e^{O_0^1},e^{O_1^1}...e^{O_{s/k-1}^1}|...|e^{O_0^1},e^{O_1^1}...e^{O_{s/k-1}^1}
$$

$$
\vdots
$$

$$
e^{O_0^{t''-1}},e^{O_1^{t''-1}}...e^{O_{s/k-1}^{t''-1}}|...|e^{O_0^{t''-1}},e^{O_1^{t''-1}}...e^{O_{s/k-1}^{t''-1}}
$$

对上述t''个密文相加后得
$$
\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_0^i},\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_1^i}...\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_{s/k-1}^i}|\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_0^i},\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_1^i}...\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_{s/k-1}^i}|....|\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_0^i},\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_1^i}...\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_{s/k-1}^i}
$$
计算softmax
$$
P_0=
\\
\frac{e^{O_0^0}}{\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_0^i}},\frac{e^{O_1^0}}{\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_1^i}}...\frac{e^{O_1^0}}{\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_{s/k-1}^i}}|...|...每组都相同
$$
$\frac{e^{O_0^0}}{\sum_{i=0}^{t''-1}e^{O_0^i}}$表示样本0在第0个类别的概率，记为$p_0^0$
$$
P_0=[p_0^0,p_1^0,p_2^0...p_{s/k-1}^0|...|p_0^0,p_1^0,p_2^0...p_{s/k-1}^0]每组都相同
\\
P_1=[p_0^1,p_1^1,p_2^1...p_{s/k-1}^1|...|p_0^1,p_1^1,p_2^1...p_{s/k-1}^1]
\\
\vdots
\\
P_{t''-1}=[p_0^{t''-1},p_1^{t''-1},p_2^{t''-1}...p_{s/k-1}^{t''-1}|...|p_0^{t''-1},p_1^{t''-1},p_2^{t''-1}...p_{s/k-1}^{t''-1}]
$$
对每个样本都需要计算交叉熵损失函数，第m个样本的交叉熵损失$L_m=-\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}y_m^jlog(p_m^j)$

首先需要对标签进行编码，明文状态的one-hot编码$y_m=[y_m^0,y_m^1...y_m^{t''}],y_m^j=1当且仅当m属于类别j$
$$
Y_0={y_0^0,y_1^0,y_2^0...y_{s/k-1}^0}|{y_0^0,y_1^0,y_2^0...y_{s/k-1}^0}|...|\\每组都相同
\\
Y_1={y_0^1,y_1^1,y_2^1...y_{s/k-1}^1}|{y_0^1,y_1^1,y_2^1...y_{s/k-1}^1}|...|
\\
\vdots
\\
Y_{t''-1}={y_0^{t''-1},y_1^{t''-1},y_2^{t''-1}...y_{s/k-1}^{t''-1}}|{y_0^{t''-1},y_1^{t''-1},y_2^{t''-1}...y_{s/k-1}^{t''-1}}|...|
$$

对$P_i$取对数后计算交叉熵损失函数
$$
-\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}(Y^j\odot log(P^j))
$$
得到
$$
L=[L_0,L_1...L_{s/k-1}|...|L_0,L_1...L_{s/k-1}]
$$
组内求和，旋转$log_2{s/k}$可得
$$
[\bar{L},\bar{L},\bar{L},\bar{L}...\bar{L},\bar{L}]
$$

## 后向传播

### 输出层梯度

由于使用交叉熵损失和Softmax激活函数，第m个样本在第j个输出神经元的梯度为： $$\delta_{m}^j = p_m^j - y_m^j$$

在密文状态下，第j个输出类别的梯度密文为： $$\Delta^j = P^j - Y^j$$

展开形式： $$\Delta^j=[\delta_0^j,\delta_1^j,....\delta_{s/k-1}^j|\delta_0^j,\delta_1^j,....\delta_{s/k-1}^j|\delta_0^j,\delta_1^j,....\delta_{s/k-1}^j......]$$

### 输出层权重梯度

对于权重$W_{j,l}$（连接隐藏层第l个神经元到输出层第j个神经元），其梯度为： $$\frac{\partial \bar{L}}{\partial W_{j,l}} = \frac{1}{s/k} \sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1} \delta_m^j \cdot A_m^l$$

**来源**：

- $\bar{L} = \frac{1}{s/k}\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1} L_m$（平均损失定义）
- 链式法则：$\frac{\partial L_m}{\partial W_{j,l}} = \frac{\partial L_m}{\partial O_m^j} \cdot \frac{\partial O_m^j}{\partial W_{j,l}}$
- 其中$\frac{\partial L_m}{\partial O_m^j} = \delta_m^j$，$\frac{\partial O_m^j}{\partial W_{j,l}} = A_m^l$

计算前k列权重的梯度$G_{0,{0:k-1}}$表示输出层的第0个神经元的0～k-1列权重的单样本梯度
$$
G_{0,{0:k-1}}=\Delta^0\odot A_0
\\
=
[\delta_0^0A_0^0,\delta_1^0A_1^0...\delta_{s/k-1}^0A_{s/k-1}^0|\delta_0^0A_0^1,\delta_1^0A_1^0...\delta_{s/k-1}^0A_{s/k-1}^1|...|\delta_0^0A_0^{k-1},\delta_1^0A_1^{k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0A_{s/k-1}^{k-1}]
$$
同理可得

$G_{0,{k:2k-1}}=\Delta^0\odot A_0,G_{0,{2k:3k-1}}=\Delta^0\odot A_1...G_{0,{t'-k-1:t'-1}}=\Delta^0\odot A_{t'/k-1}$

将每个G都进行组内求和：
$$
S_{0,{0:k-1}}=[\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^0,\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^0...|\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^1,\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^1...|......|\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^{k-1},\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^{k-1}...\sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1}\delta_m^0A_m^{k-1}]
$$
同理得

$S_{0,{k:2k-1}},S_{0,{2k:3k-1}}...S_{0,{t'-k-t:t'-1}}$

除以batchSize（s/k）得到梯度：$\nabla W_{0,0:k-1} = \frac{1}{s/k} \cdot S_{0,0:k-1}$

同理得到其余的梯度



### 隐藏层梯度

==为方便隐藏层和输出层的梯度都用$\delta$表示==

对于隐藏层第l个神经元，第m个样本的梯度为： $$\delta_m^l = \phi'(Z_m^l) \cdot \sum_{j=0}^{t''-1} \delta_m^{output,j} \cdot W_{j,l}$$

其中：

- $\phi'(Z_m^l)$是激活函数在$Z_m^l$处的导数
- $Z_m^l$是隐藏层第l个神经元的加权输入（激活前）
- $W$是隐藏层到输出层的权重

在密文下
$$
\frac{\partial{L_m}}{\partial{Z_m^l}}=\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\frac{\partial{L_m}}{\partial{O_m^j}}\frac{\partial{O_m^j}}{\partial{A_m^l}}\frac{\partial{A_m^l}}{\partial{Z_m^l}}
$$
即
$$
\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_m^jW_{j,l}\phi'(Z_m^l)
$$
在密文下计算：
$$
R_{0,0:k-1}=[\delta_0^0W_{0,0},\delta_1^0W_{0,0}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{0,0}|\delta_0^0W_{0,1},\delta_1^0W_{0,1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{0,1}|...|\delta_0^0W_{0,k-1},\delta_1^0W_{0,k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{0,k-1}]
\\
R_{1,0:k-1}=[\delta_0^0W_{1,0},\delta_1^0W_{1,0}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{1,0}|\delta_0^0W_{1,1},\delta_1^0W_{1,1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{1,1}|\delta_0^0W_{1,k-1},\delta_1^0W_{1,k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{1,k-1}]
\\
R_{2,0:k-1}=[\delta_0^0W_{2,0},\delta_1^0W_{2,0}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{2,0}|\delta_0^0W_{2,1},\delta_1^0W_{2,1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{2,1}|\delta_0^0W_{2,k-1},\delta_1^0W_{2,k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{2,k-1}]
\\
R_{3,0:k-1}=[\delta_0^0W_{3,0},\delta_1^0W_{3,0}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{3,0}|\delta_0^0W_{3,1},\delta_1^0W_{3,1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{3,1}|\delta_0^0W_{3,k-1},\delta_1^0W_{3,k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{3,k-1}]
\\
\vdots
\\
R_{t''-1,0:k-1}=[\delta_0^0W_{t''-1,0},\delta_1^0W_{t''-1,0}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{t''-1,0}|\delta_0^0W_{t''-1,1},\delta_1^0W_{t''-1,1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{t''-1,1}|\delta_0^0W_{t''-1,k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^0W_{t''-1,k-1}]
\\
$$
上述t''个密文$\oplus$得：
$$
[\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_0^jW_{j,0},\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_1^jW_{j,0}...\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_{s/k-1}^jW_{j,0}|\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_0^jW_{j,1},...\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_{s/k-1}^jW_{j,1}|...|\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_0^jW_{j,k-1},\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_1^jW_{j,k-1}...\sum_{j=0}^{t''-1}\delta_{s/k-1}^jW_{j,k-1}]
$$
激活函数的导数
$$
\phi'(Z_0^0),\phi'(Z_1^0)...\phi'(Z_{s/k-1}^0)|\phi'(Z_0^1),\phi'(Z_1^1)...\phi'(Z_{s/k-1}^1)|...|\phi'(Z_0{k-1}),\phi'(Z_1^{k-1})...\phi'(Z_{s/k-1}^{k-1})
$$
对应相乘可以得到隐藏层前k个神经元的梯度$\Gamma_{0:k-1}^h$

同理我们可以得到隐藏层所有神经元的梯度$\Gamma_{k:2k-1}^h,\Gamma_{2k:3k-1}^h$

共有t'/k个



### 隐藏层权重梯度计算

对于隐藏层权重$W_{i,f}$（连接输入第f个特征到隐藏层第i个神经元），其梯度为： $$\frac{\partial \bar{L}}{\partial W_{i,f}} = \frac{1}{s/k} \sum_{m=0}^{s/k-1} \delta_{hidden,m}^i \cdot X_m[f]$$

隐藏层的梯度计算结果如下
$$
\Gamma^h_{0:k-1}=[\delta_0^0,\delta_1^0...\delta_{s/k-1}^0|\delta_0^1,\delta_1^1...\delta_{s/k-1}^1|...|\delta_0^{k-1},\delta_1^{k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^{k-1}]
$$
利用掩码提取出上式中k个隐藏层神经元的梯度如下：
$$
\Gamma^h_0=\delta_0^0,\delta_1^0...\delta_{s/k-1}^0|\delta_0^0,\delta_1^0...\delta_{s/k-1}^0|...\delta_0^0,\delta_1^0...\delta_{s/k-1}^0
\\
\Gamma^h_1=\delta_0^1,\delta_1^1...\delta_{s/k-1}^1|\delta_0^1,\delta_1^1...\delta_{s/k-1}^1|...\delta_0^1,\delta_1^1...\delta_{s/k-1}^1
\\
\vdots
\\
\Gamma^h_{k-1}=\delta_0^{k-1},\delta_1^{k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^{k-1}|\delta_0^{k-1}\delta_1^{k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^{k-1}|...\delta_0^{k-1},\delta_1^{k-1}...\delta_{s/k-1}^{k-1}
$$
